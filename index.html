AI: Quo vadis?

Some of my beliefs when it comes to AI. Come back in a few days, they might have changed.

  1. The importance of Observability has only increased. With humans losing their institutionalised knowledge of code (written by AI), Application Performance Monitoring (APM) is key to have proper understanding of your system.1
  2. AI Security continues to be an unsolved topic. Since guardrails and other current approaches to AI security are unreliable2, conventional security gets even more important: Principle of least privilege, separation of concerns, shifting left (and probably not even relying on authentication too much3).
  3. We're all figuring this out.4 Yes, you can vibe code a Figma clone in two weeks. Don't get me wrong, this is seriously impressive. But it still isn't Figma. In the past, we had at least a year or two to let shiny and hyped things settle. These days, everyone is trying to use AI anytime, anywhere - and I think we haven't even reached the trough of disillusionment.5
  4. Learning is more important than ever. And harder. Not just learning how to use AI effectively, but keeping fundamentals sharp enough to catch its mistakes, and staying critical enough to know when it's subtly wrong. Prompting and hoping is not a strategy.

Some of my own explorations with AI: MacOS Tools, a map displaying the accessibility of Bamberg's streets, and a helper to figure out which AI agent is the best for your use case.
